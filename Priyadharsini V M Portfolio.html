About

I work where quality and delivery meet: translating requirements into test scenarios, giving product and engineering early feedback on risk, and telling the team plainly whether a release is ready.

Most recently I held overall responsibility for release quality at Cin7Core, a cloud inventory, order and warehouse platform that connects to e-commerce marketplaces, POS and retail channels, and accounting systems.

Earlier years were spent leading onsite and offshore test teams on banking, insurance, web and mobile programmes at Cognizant, in both Agile (Scrum) and Waterfall delivery.

I coach QA and engineering teammates on test design and automation, and I hold the line on reliability standards, process compliance and quality gates.

I coordinate System Integration Testing and UAT across multiple client and release teams, managing dependencies, defect triage and readiness sign-off.
